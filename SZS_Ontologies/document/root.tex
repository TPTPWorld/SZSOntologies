\title{The SZS Ontologies}
\author{Johannes Schuster \and Geoff Sutcliffe}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The SZS success ontology~\cite{Sut08,SZS} is formalised: each of its
forty-five model theoretic values is a condition on the models of an axiom
set and a conjecture, and the counter values are obtained from the positive
ones by a mirror theorem.  Entailment between values is decided by reducing
the two model sets to one of 81 finite shapes; the decision procedure is
proved sound and complete.  The isa hierarchy is the transitive reduction of
the entailment order, and the BNF of the success ontology is generated from
it.  Each edge of the hierarchy is exported as a TPTP problem in TF0 and
TH0~\cite{TFF0,THF0}, with a proof that the problem states the corresponding
entailment.
\end{abstract}
